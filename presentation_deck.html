Slide 4/5 • Adaptive SNE in Action (1:10 - 1:35)

6 Tailored SNE Pathways

Pedagogically grounded accommodations for real classroom disabilities.

👂 Hearing Impairment
Kenyan Sign Language (KSL) glosses, 3-color desk cards, and semi-circular horseshoe seating (0% auditory).
📖 Dyslexia
Visual storyboards, single-line reading cutout windows, color-coded place values, zero spelling penalty.
🧮 Dyscalculia
Concrete CPA progression, desk Ten-Frames, step-by-step formula flip cards, zero timed math drills.
⚡ ADHD & Focus
3-min micro-sprints, desktop visual countdown timers, active squad roles (Timekeeper/Material Captain).
♿ Motor Challenges
20° slant boards, adapted grips, and 'Pilot & Scribe' peer protocol (Pilot directs, peer scribe writes).
👁️ Blind / Low Vision
Pre-knotted measuring ropes (5 cm tactile knots), embossed edges, and audio descriptions.
Speaker Spoken Target (25 Seconds): "What does the teacher receive? Genuine pedagogical adaptation.
